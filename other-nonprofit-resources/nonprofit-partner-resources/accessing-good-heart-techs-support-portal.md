# Accessing Good Heart Tech's Support Portal

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Partners only.** These steps apply only if your nonprofit already receives free IT from Good Heart Tech. Public visitors: see [Free IT Support from Good Heart Tech](../../free-it-support-from-good-heart-tech.md).
{% endhint %}

Use our support portal to submit tickets, review updates, and view your IT inventory, dashboards, and reports.

👉 [https://support.goodhearttech.org](https://support.goodhearttech.org/)

### Sign in

Log in with your organization's **Google** _or_ **Microsoft 365** account. No separate password required.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### What you can do in the portal

The home screen has four shortcuts: **My Tickets**, **My Dashboards**, **My Reports**, and **Summary of Services**.

* **My Tickets** — open, update, and review support requests
* **My Dashboards** — live views of tickets and other data you have access to
* **My Reports** — PDFs and lists we keep current for your organization, including:
  * A custom business continuity and disaster recovery (BCDR) plan
  * A tech punch list of outstanding action items
  * Domain health (DNS, email, and related security)
  * Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace / identity-provider audits
  * Website health and security
  * Asset inventory, active users, phishing-awareness status, and a monthly summary of services
* **Summary of Services** — a snapshot of the work Good Heart Tech has provided to your nonprofit, including volunteer hours

Not every report is available on day one. If something is missing or looks out of date, [submit a ticket](how-to-submit-a-support-ticket-to-good-heart-tech.md).

### Need help logging in?

Email us at [support@goodhearttech.org](mailto:support@goodhearttech.org).
