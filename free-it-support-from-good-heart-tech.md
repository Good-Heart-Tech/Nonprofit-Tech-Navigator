# Free IT Support from Good Heart Tech

Good Heart Tech is a volunteer-run **501(c)(3) nonprofit** (EIN **84-3156575**, founded **2019**, Boise, Idaho) that provides **free managed IT (MSP-style) services** at **$0** to qualifying nonprofit partners in the United States.

## What we provide (free for eligible partners)

- Ongoing IT support in an MSP model: helpdesk, security, cloud administration, backups, and consulting
- Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace planning, migration, and security
- WordPress maintenance, phishing simulation, SaaS backups, and practical cybersecurity improvements
- Remote support nationwide for most services

## Eligibility (free tier)

- Fewer than **50** paid staff
- Less than **$2,000,000** in external/government funding per year
- 501(c)(3) nonprofits that meet our partner criteria (we exclude certain org types — see FAQ)

## Limitations

- Volunteer-run: **no SLAs** or guaranteed **24/7** support
- We can refer organizations needing enterprise SLAs to commercial MSPs
