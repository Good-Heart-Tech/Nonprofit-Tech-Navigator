# Employee Onboarding & Offboarding (IT Checklist)

When someone **joins** or **leaves**, the same IT steps should happen every time. This page is Good Heart Tech’s **suggested list**. Your nonprofit still chooses which steps apply.

{% hint style="success" %}
**Keep your real checklist inside your organization.** Copy what you need into **one place your team already uses**—for example Google Drive or Shared drive, SharePoint, your employee handbook, or HR folder—so anyone can **search** and find it quickly. Title it something obvious like *Staff IT onboarding/offboarding*. This NTN page is a reference; **your** document is the source of truth for *your* steps, names, and timing.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
HR, legal, or grants may require extra steps or different timing (same-day shutoff vs. two weeks of forwarded email). **Good Heart Tech partners:** we can help, but your org makes the final calls and owns admin accounts. [Open a support ticket](../../other-nonprofit-resources/nonprofit-partner-resources/how-to-submit-a-support-ticket-to-good-heart-tech.md) when you want hands-on help.
{% endhint %}

## Onboarding (new staff or volunteer)

**Goal:** They can work on day one—and they only get access they actually need.

| Step | In plain English |
| --- | --- |
| **Work email** | Create their Google or Microsoft account. Use a consistent format (see [naming tips](user-naming-conventions-for-email-and-identity-systems.md)). |
| **Sign-in safety** | Turn on **two-step verification (MFA)** before they read email or open sensitive files. |
| **Groups & files** | Add them only to mailing lists, team folders, and apps their role needs—not “everything.” |
| **Shared inboxes** | For **info@**-style mail, give access through **delegation** or a shared mailbox—never share one password. |
| **Computer** | Set up org equipment (updates, antivirus). GHT partners: install [support tools](../../other-nonprofit-resources/nonprofit-partner-resources/install-good-heart-techs-support-tools-on-your-computer.md) if you use them. |
| **Other logins** | CRM, website, payroll, password manager—create accounts by role, not “admin for all.” |
| **Basics** | Point them to your policies and phishing tips. If they use a personal PC for work, use a [separate browser profile](../../tech-guides/software-guides/how-to-use-separate-chrome-and-edge-profiles-for-work-and-personal.md). |

**Examples**

* **Program manager:** Email account, team folder, no finance access until approved.
* **Part-time volunteer lead:** Email account, access to **volunteers@** list only, MFA on first login.

## Offboarding (someone is leaving)

**Goal:** They **cannot log in** anymore. The organization still has email and files where you need them. Work with HR on **when** (last day vs. immediate).

| Step | In plain English |
| --- | --- |
| **Stop login** | **Google:** suspend the user. **Microsoft:** block sign-in. Sign them out everywhere if you can. |
| **Passwords & keys** | Reset password if your policy says so. Collect YubiKeys or other MFA devices. Turn off extra app passwords. |
| **Email** | Forward or give a manager access for a **set time**. Use an out-of-office message if helpful. Remove them as delegate on **other** people’s mail. |
| **Files** | Move ownership of important Google Drive or OneDrive/SharePoint files to a manager. |
| **Remove from lists** | Google Groups, Teams, shared folders, calendars, chat—take them off and fix anything they **owned**. |
| **Licenses** | When mail is handled, **free the license** so you are not paying for an unused account. |
| **Devices** | Get org laptops back. Remove the computer from your inventory tools. |
| **Other apps** | Turn off CRM, website admin, fundraising tools, social logins, etc. Change shared passwords they knew. |
| **Write it down** | Note what you did and who took over their work (in your org’s checklist or ticket system). |

**Examples**

* **Director leaving:** Suspend email same day; manager gets mail for 90 days; board folder ownership moves to the chair.
* **Intern done:** Suspend account; remove from all groups; no forward unless the supervisor asks.
* **Urgent exit:** Suspend immediately; no forward to personal email; HR decides if mail must be saved.

### Google vs. Microsoft (admin names)

| What you need | Google Workspace | Microsoft 365 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Turn off access | **Suspend user** | **Block sign-in** |
| Hand off email | Delegate or forward | Shared mailbox or forward |
| Hand off files | Transfer Drive owner | Transfer OneDrive / adjust SharePoint |
| Save a license | Suspend, then archive/delete when ready | [Unassign license](../../microsoft/microsoft-365-nonprofit-setup/managing-nonprofit-licenses-in-the-microsoft-365-admin-center.md) |

## Make it yours (inside your org)

1. Copy the tables above into **your** handbook or shared drive.
2. Check only the rows you will actually use.
3. Name **who** does IT steps (office manager, ED, volunteer IT).
4. Agree with HR **when** offboarding runs.
5. Review once a year—volunteer accounts and shared inboxes are easy to forget.
