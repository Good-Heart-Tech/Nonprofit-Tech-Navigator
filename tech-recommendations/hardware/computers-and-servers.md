# Computers & Servers

For nonprofits, procuring computers and other tech equipment can be challenging on a budget. Below, we outline some great resources for finding low-cost or free computers.

{% hint style="success" %}
**If you only pick one:** Buy a Windows 11 Pro laptop with 16 GB RAM and a 512 GB SSD. TechSoup, Dell Refurbished, and the [CTL Reuse Store](https://reuse.ctl.net/) (3R Technology) are the usual starting points.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
We only recommend purchasing Windows 11 computers. [Windows 10 became obsolete in October 2025](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/end-of-support?r=1).
{% endhint %}

## Computer minimum requirements (recommended)

We recommend only buying PCs that meet these minimum specs:

* **Windows version:** **Windows 11**, not Windows 10. [Windows 10 became obsolete in October 2025](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/end-of-support?r=1).
* **Windows edition:** **Windows 11 Pro** (not Home)
  * Home cannot join Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) or an Active Directory domain.
  * Home often ships with extra consumer apps (“bloatware”).
  * If you must buy Windows 10, get **Windows 10 Pro** (not Home).
* **RAM:** 16 GB minimum
* **Storage:** 512 GB **SSD** minimum
* **Display**: Has a screen that's at least 1080p (1920 x 1080 pixels, AKA Full HD)

### Docking stations (dual monitors, mixed laptops)

A **universal dock** helps when you swap between **Dell**, **HP**, and **Mac** laptops at a desk—one cable for monitors, Ethernet, keyboard, and mouse. Good Heart Tech often suggests **Plugable** docks in the **~$100–$150** range: solid for office work, widely used in nonprofits, and supported by a US-based help team.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Mac note:** These docks use **DisplayLink** for dual monitors. Install [Plugable’s Mac driver](https://plugable.com/displaylink) once per Mac (Windows usually installs via Windows Update). They are for **email, spreadsheets, and video calls**—not for protected streaming (Netflix, etc.) or gaming.
{% endhint %}

## Computer Sources

Here are some of the places you may consider looking for PCs for your nonprofit:

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Avoid Walmart, Costco, Sam’s Club, and similar big-box “doorbuster” PCs** when you can. Those systems often ship with **Windows 11 Home**, trial software, and other **bloatware** that slows the computer and makes support harder. Prefer business-grade or refurb sources below.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Amazon is okay** if the listing clearly matches the [minimum requirements](#computer-minimum-requirements-recommended) above—especially **Windows 11 Pro**, **16 GB RAM**, and a **512 GB SSD**. Home edition and bundled junk apps are common on Amazon too, so read the specs before you buy.
{% endhint %}
