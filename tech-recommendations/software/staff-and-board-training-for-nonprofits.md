# Staff & Board Training for Nonprofits

Training keeps staff, volunteers, and board members confident and compliant, but paid learning platforms are often out of reach for small nonprofits. This page covers free e-learning options built for **nonprofit staff and board training only**. It is not a tool for training your customers, clients, or the public.

{% hint style="success" %}
**If you only pick one:** OWL (Open World Learning). It is free for eligible US and Canadian nonprofits and includes ready-made courses for staff and board members.
{% endhint %}

#### OWL (Open World Learning)

[OWL](https://www.join.owl.inc) is a free e-learning platform for small registered charities and nonprofits in the US and Canada. It is powered by Docebo and funded by Intercap Impact, so there is no platform or license fee. It is meant for training your own **staff, volunteers, and board members**.

**What you get for free:**

* A library of ready-made courses on HR compliance, DEI, mental health, board governance, leadership, and professional development.
* One person at your nonprofit (the "Charity Lead") creates the account and shares access with staff, volunteers, and board members by uploading a CSV.
* Mobile apps for iOS and Android.

**Good to know:**

* **The free tier does not support custom content.** You can take the courses in the library, but you cannot build or upload your own. OWL says nonprofits with their own materials can apply for a separate branded account through the Upgrade Account option on the OWL dashboard. Ask OWL about cost and features before you count on it.
* **Eligibility:** Registered US or Canadian charities and nonprofits with fewer than 300 active users per month. Approval can take a few weeks.
* **Funding:** OWL is funded by Intercap Impact, so long-term availability depends on that funding continuing.

{% hint style="info" %}
Need training on your own policies, such as your board orientation or an internal handbook? OWL's free library will not cover that on its own. Ask [Good Heart Tech](../../free-it-support-from-good-heart-tech.md) and we can help you find the right option.
{% endhint %}
