# Staff & Board Training for Nonprofits

Free online courses to train your own staff, volunteers, and board members. This is for internal nonprofit training only, not for training clients or the public.

{% hint style="success" %}
**If you only pick one:** OWL. It is free for eligible US and Canadian nonprofits.
{% endhint %}

#### OWL (Open World Learning)

[OWL](https://www.join.owl.inc) is a free training platform for small nonprofits, funded by Intercap Impact. It comes with ready-made courses on HR compliance, DEI, mental health, board governance, leadership, and professional development. One person sets up the account and adds everyone else.

* **Cost:** Free.
* **Who qualifies:** Registered US and Canadian nonprofits with fewer than 300 active users per month. Approval can take a few weeks.
* **Custom content:** Not on the free plan. You can take OWL's courses but not upload your own. OWL offers a separate branded account for that, so ask them about cost first.
* **Heads up:** Free access depends on OWL's funding continuing.
